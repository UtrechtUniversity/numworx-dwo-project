For building a release please first built a release DWOJApplet with activated the profiles
dwo-obfuscate and dwo-pack-sign manually. This will produce a DWOJApplet-*-with-dependencies.jar
which is the only jar that is signed and obfuscated. Then building DWOServer will
copy the signed and obfuscated jar into the war for  production release. 

Verify the context.xml for the proper jars, resources and apps location.
